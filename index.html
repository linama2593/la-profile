About

I'm a data scientist with two years of applied machine learning experience, most recently as the sole data scientist at Periphery Security, a cybersecurity startup, where I built production anomaly-detection and threat-classification pipelines and an LLM-orchestrated compliance automation tool. Before that I spent six years in academic research, first as a PhD student and then as a postdoctoral researcher, working on causal inference and applied econometrics in education and public health policy.

I like owning a problem end to end: framing the question, building the pipeline or model, and then explaining the result to whoever needs to act on it, whether that's an engineering team, a CEO, or a room of civil servants. That habit came from research, where a result only matters once someone outside your own head understands why it's true, and it carried straight over into industry.
